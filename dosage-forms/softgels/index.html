What Are Nutraceutical Softgels?

Softgels: The Superior Delivery System for Oil-Based Supplements

Softgel capsules are single-piece, hermetically sealed dosage forms consisting of a gelatin or plant-based outer shell surrounding a liquid or semi-solid fill matrix. Unlike hard-shell capsules that encase dry powders, softgels encapsulate oil-based formulations — making them the definitive delivery system for fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K), essential fatty acids (Omega-3 EPA and DHA), and lipophilic bioactives (CoQ10, Ubiquinol, astaxanthin, lutein). The encapsulation process uses rotary die technology that simultaneously forms, fills, and seals each softgel in a single continuous operation.

The hermetic seal of a softgel protects oxygen-sensitive oils from oxidative degradation, extending shelf life and preserving potency. Because the active ingredient is pre-dissolved or suspended in an oil carrier, the body can absorb it more rapidly than from a dry powder tablet or capsule. This pharmacokinetic advantage is particularly significant for poorly water-soluble nutrients that rely on lipid-based absorption pathways in the small intestine. Softgels can be manufactured in oblong, oval, round, and custom shapes, with sizes ranging from minigels (3 minim) to large-fill formats (24 minim for high-dose fish oil).

Softgel capsules improve the bioavailability of fat-soluble nutrients by up to 3x compared to standard tablets, making them the preferred delivery system for oil-based supplements.

Product Overview

Why Softgels for Oil-Based Nutrients?

Oil-based nutraceutical ingredients present a fundamental formulation challenge: they cannot be compressed into tablets or filled into hard capsules in their native liquid state. Softgel encapsulation solves this problem elegantly by suspending the active in a carrier oil (typically soybean, sunflower, or MCT oil) and sealing it within a flexible gelatin or vegetarian shell.

This approach delivers three critical advantages over alternative dosage forms. First, pre-dissolution of the active ingredient in a lipid carrier eliminates the need for the body to perform this step during digestion, accelerating onset of absorption. Second, the oxygen-barrier properties of the softgel shell protect polyunsaturated fatty acids like EPA and DHA from oxidative rancidity — a problem that plagues fish oil tablets and liquid supplements. Third, softgels offer superior dose uniformity because liquid fills can be metered with volumetric precision, ensuring every unit contains the exact labelled amount.

Key Nutrient Categories for Softgels

  • Omega-3 Fatty Acids — Fish oil (EPA + DHA), algal DHA, krill oil, and flaxseed oil in concentrations from 500 mg to 1,500 mg per softgel.
  • Fat-Soluble Vitamins — Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol), Vitamin E (d-alpha tocopherol), Vitamin K2 (MK-7), and Vitamin A (retinyl palmitate).
  • Coenzyme Q10 & Ubiquinol — Both oxidised CoQ10 and reduced Ubiquinol (Kaneka) in 50 mg to 200 mg dosages.
  • Specialty Oils — Evening Primrose Oil, Borage Oil, Black Seed Oil, Sea Buckthorn Oil, and conjugated linoleic acid (CLA).
  • Carotenoids — Astaxanthin, lutein (FloraGLO), zeaxanthin, lycopene, and beta-carotene in oil-suspended matrices.

Manufacturing Capabilities

End-to-End Softgel Manufacturing at Bionial

Our WHO-GMP certified facility in Mohali is equipped with dedicated softgel manufacturing lines covering every stage from oil preparation through finished goods packaging. Nitrogen blanketing throughout the process protects oxygen-sensitive oils.

Oil Preparation & Mixing

Temperature-controlled mixing vessels homogenise active oils with carrier lipids, emulsifiers, and antioxidants under nitrogen atmosphere to prevent oxidation.

Rotary Die Encapsulation

Precision rotary die machines simultaneously form gelatin ribbons, inject the measured fill, and seal each softgel — producing up to 80,000 units per hour per line.

Tumble Drying & Curing

Freshly formed softgels undergo controlled drying in tumble dryers at regulated temperature and humidity to achieve target shell hardness and moisture content.

Enteric Coating

Optional enteric coating applied via pan-coating technology for fish oil and acid-sensitive formulations, eliminating reflux and ensuring intestinal release.

Visual Inspection & Sorting

Every batch undergoes manual and automated visual inspection for cosmetic defects, leakers, and out-of-specification units before packaging.

Blister & Bottle Packaging

Alu-alu blisters for moisture-sensitive softgels, HDPE bottles with desiccant caps, and strip packing — all configured for maximum shelf-life preservation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Softgel Manufacturing FAQ

What nutrients are best suited for softgel delivery?

Softgels are ideal for oil-based and fat-soluble nutrients including Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA and DHA from fish oil or algal sources), Vitamin D3, Vitamin E, Vitamin K2, CoQ10 and Ubiquinol, Evening Primrose Oil, Flaxseed Oil, conjugated linoleic acid (CLA), astaxanthin, and lutein. The liquid fill matrix protects oxygen-sensitive oils from degradation and improves bioavailability by up to 3x compared to dry powder forms.

Can Bionial manufacture enteric-coated softgels?

Yes. Bionial manufactures enteric-coated softgels that resist dissolution in the stomach (pH below 5.5) and release their contents in the intestine. This is particularly important for fish oil softgels to eliminate fishy burps and reflux, for high-dose omega-3 formulations, and for acid-sensitive nutrients. Our enteric coating uses pharmaceutical-grade polymers that maintain integrity in gastric acid while dissolving reliably at intestinal pH.

What is the minimum order quantity for softgel manufacturing?

Bionial offers a minimum order quantity starting from 1,000 units per SKU for softgel manufacturing. This makes us accessible to D2C supplement brands, clinical nutrition companies, and startups launching new oil-based supplement products. For larger orders, we offer volume-based pricing with batch sizes scaled to your market requirements. Standard turnaround is 30 days from purchase order to dispatch.

Can you manufacture Kaneka Ubiquinol softgels?

Yes. Bionial is authorised to formulate with Kaneka Ubiquinol — the world's most researched and clinically validated reduced form of Coenzyme Q10. We manufacture Ubiquinol softgels in dosages from 50 mg to 200 mg, with optional additions of Vitamin E, Selenium, L-Carnitine, and other synergistic nutrients. Kaneka Ubiquinol offers 3-8x higher bioavailability compared to conventional oxidised CoQ10, making it essential for cardiac, energy, and anti-aging formulations.

Are vegetarian softgels available?

Yes. In addition to traditional bovine and porcine gelatin softgels, Bionial manufactures vegetarian softgels using modified starch (plant-derived) and carrageenan-based shells. Vegetarian softgels are suitable for vegan, halal, and kosher product lines. They are available for algal DHA, Vitamin D3 (from lichen), Vitamin E, and other plant-sourced oil-based nutrients. The fill formulation uses non-animal carrier oils such as sunflower or MCT oil.
