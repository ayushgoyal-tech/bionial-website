What Are Nutraceutical Tablets?

Tablets: The Most Versatile Dosage Form in Nutraceutical Manufacturing

Tablets are compressed solid dosage forms manufactured by applying high mechanical pressure to a granulated or directly compressible blend of active ingredients and excipients. In nutraceutical manufacturing, tablets offer unmatched versatility — they can be formulated as immediate-release, sustained-release, extended-release, chewable, effervescent, sublingual, or orally disintegrating formats. The compression process uses rotary tablet presses equipped with precision punch-and-die tooling that determines the tablet's shape, size, weight, hardness, and surface embossing.

Tablets can incorporate a wider range of active ingredient loads than any other oral solid dosage form, from micro-dose melatonin tablets at 0.5 mg to large-format multivitamin caplets exceeding 1,500 mg. Post-compression, tablets can receive functional coatings — including film coats for moisture protection and brand aesthetics, enteric coats for intestinal targeting, and sustained-release coats for prolonged drug delivery over 8 to 12 hours. This flexibility, combined with lower per-unit manufacturing costs and superior physical stability during storage and transport, makes tablets the dominant dosage form in the Indian nutraceutical industry.

Tablets remain the most cost-effective nutraceutical dosage form, with manufacturing costs 20-30% lower than capsules while offering superior stability and longer shelf life.

Tablet Types

Complete Range of Nutraceutical Tablet Formats

Bionial manufactures every major category of nutraceutical tablet, giving your brand the flexibility to choose the optimal format for each product line, target audience, and therapeutic objective.

Film-Coated Tablets

The industry standard. Thin polymer film (HPMC-based) protects the core from moisture and light, masks taste and odour, and enables custom colour branding. Ideal for multivitamins, calcium, iron, and herbal extracts.

Sugar-Coated Tablets

Multiple layers of sugar-based coating create a smooth, glossy finish with complete taste masking. Suited for bitter Ayurvedic extracts and pediatric-friendly formulations where palatability is critical.

Chewable Tablets

Formulated with food-grade sweeteners (sucralose, stevia, or sugar), flavours, and texturising agents. Available in orange, strawberry, mixed berry, mango, and mint. Ideal for calcium, antacids, and children's vitamins.

Effervescent Tablets

Acid-base reaction (citric acid + sodium bicarbonate) creates a fizzy, pleasant-tasting drink when dissolved in water. Superior absorption for Vitamin C, electrolytes, iron, and multivitamin formulations.

Sustained-Release (SR)

Hydrophilic HPMC matrix controls release rate over 8-12 hours. Reduces dosing frequency and minimises GI side effects. Used for iron, B-vitamins, magnesium, and melatonin.

Extended-Release (ER)

Hydrophobic wax matrix or multi-particulate system for zero-order release kinetics. Maintains steady blood levels for up to 24 hours. Ideal for high-compliance supplement regimens.

Bi-Layer Tablets

Two distinct layers in a single tablet — one immediate-release and one sustained-release, or two incompatible actives separated physically. Advanced rotary press technology.

Orally Disintegrating (ODT)

Disintegrates on the tongue within 30 seconds without water. Superdisintegrants and freeze-dried matrices enable rapid dissolution. Ideal for on-the-go consumers and elderly patients.

Product Overview

Why Choose Tablets for Your Nutraceutical Line?

Tablets offer four fundamental advantages that make them the first choice for the majority of nutraceutical product launches in India. First, cost efficiency: tablet compression is inherently faster and less expensive than encapsulation or softgel manufacturing, translating to 20-30% lower per-unit costs that directly improve your margins. Second, stability: the dense compressed matrix of a tablet provides excellent resistance to moisture, oxygen, and mechanical stress during shipping and storage.

Third, dose flexibility: a single tablet press can produce formats ranging from 100 mg micro-tablets to 1,500 mg large-format caplets, accommodating everything from melatonin micro-doses to high-load calcium and multivitamin products. Fourth, consumer recognition: tablets remain the most familiar supplement format among Indian consumers, requiring no explanation at point of sale and enjoying the highest compliance rates across all age groups.

Manufacturing Capabilities

End-to-End Tablet Manufacturing at Bionial

Our WHO-GMP certified facility in Mohali, Punjab is equipped for complete tablet manufacturing from raw material receipt through finished goods packaging. Every stage operates under strict in-process controls with full batch documentation.

Granulation & Blending

Wet granulation (rapid mixer granulator + FBD), dry granulation (roller compactor), and direct compression pathways. V-blenders and octagonal blenders ensure uniform content.

Tablet Compression

High-speed rotary tablet presses with output up to 300,000 tablets per hour. Multi-station tooling for round, oval, caplet, and custom shapes with debossing capability.

Film & Enteric Coating

Perforated pan coating systems apply HPMC film coats, enteric coats (Eudragit, HPMCP), sugar coats, and sustained-release coats with uniform thickness control.

Effervescent Line

Dedicated humidity-controlled room for effervescent tablet compression, with moisture levels maintained below 25% RH throughout the manufacturing process.

Blister & Bottle Packing

Alu-alu and PVC-aluminium blister lines, HDPE bottle filling with desiccant insertion, strip packaging, and tube packaging for effervescent tablets.

Quality Control Laboratory

In-house HPLC, dissolution testing (USP Apparatus I-IV), hardness testing, friability, disintegration, weight variation, and microbial limit testing for every batch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tablet Manufacturing FAQ

What types of tablets does Bionial manufacture?

Bionial manufactures film-coated tablets, sugar-coated tablets, uncoated compressed tablets, chewable tablets, effervescent tablets, sustained-release (SR) tablets, extended-release (ER) tablets, bi-layer tablets, and orally disintegrating tablets (ODTs). Each format is optimised for specific ingredient profiles, release characteristics, and consumer preferences. We also produce custom-shaped tablets with brand debossing.

Can Bionial manufacture sustained-release and extended-release tablets?

Yes. Bionial manufactures both SR and ER tablets using hydrophilic matrix technology (HPMC-based), hydrophobic wax matrices, and multi-particulate systems. SR/ER tablets release their active ingredients gradually over 8 to 12 hours (or up to 24 hours for ER), reducing dosing frequency and improving compliance. This technology is particularly effective for iron (eliminating GI side effects), B-vitamins (sustained energy), magnesium (muscle support), and melatonin (sleep maintenance through the night).

Are chewable tablets available at Bionial?

Yes. Bionial manufactures chewable tablets in a variety of flavours including orange, strawberry, mixed berry, mango, and mint. Our chewable tablets use food-grade sweeteners (sucralose, stevia, or sugar), natural and nature-identical flavours, and texturising excipients to deliver a pleasant chewing experience. They are ideal for pediatric supplements, calcium, multivitamins, antacids, and any product targeting consumers who have difficulty swallowing standard tablets.

What is the minimum order quantity for tablet manufacturing?

Bionial offers a minimum order quantity starting from 1,000 units per SKU for tablet manufacturing. This low MOQ makes us ideal for D2C brands, supplement startups, and clinical trial batches. For larger orders, we offer volume-based pricing with batch sizes up to 1,000,000 tablets per production run. Our standard turnaround is 30 days from purchase order to dispatch, with expedited options available for urgent launches.

Can Bionial produce custom tablet shapes and sizes?

Yes. Bionial manufactures tablets in standard round, oval (caplet), and oblong shapes, as well as custom geometries using dedicated punch-and-die tooling. Tablet weights range from 100 mg (micro-tablets for pediatric use) to 1,500 mg (large-format multivitamin caplets). We also offer debossing and embossing with your brand name, logo, score lines, or product identifier on the tablet surface. Custom tooling typically requires a one-time setup charge and 2-3 weeks lead time for tool fabrication.
