Understanding the Opportunity

What Is Ayurvedic Contract Manufacturing?

Ayurvedic contract manufacturing is the process by which a brand outsources the production of Ayurvedic medicines, supplements, or wellness products to a licensed, AYUSH GMP certified facility that has the expertise, equipment, and regulatory approvals to manufacture Ayurvedic products at scale. The contract manufacturer handles everything from raw herb procurement and authentication to formulation development, batch production, quality control testing, stability studies, and finished-goods packaging — while the brand retains full ownership of the product, label, and market positioning.

This model allows Ayurvedic startups, D2C wellness brands, established pharmaceutical companies, and export houses to launch Ayurvedic products without building their own AYUSH GMP facility — saving years of capital investment and regulatory navigation. The contract manufacturer must hold valid state drug manufacturing licenses, AYUSH GMP certification from the Ministry of AYUSH, and ideally additional certifications like WHO-GMP and FSSAI to cover both drug and food-category Ayurvedic products.

India's Ayurvedic products market is valued at over $10 billion and growing at 16% CAGR, according to NITI Aayog, making AYUSH GMP compliance essential for brands entering this space.

Regulatory Foundation

AYUSH GMP Certification: What It Means for Your Brand

AYUSH GMP is the mandatory quality standard for Ayurvedic manufacturing in India. Here is what it covers, why it matters, and how Bionial's certification protects your brand from regulatory risk.

🏛️

What Is AYUSH GMP?

AYUSH GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) is a certification issued by the Ministry of AYUSH under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940. It mandates that every facility manufacturing Ayurvedic, Siddha, or Unani medicines for commercial sale must meet specific standards for premises design, equipment qualification, personnel training, raw material testing, in-process controls, finished product analysis, and batch documentation. The 2018 revised guidelines brought AYUSH GMP closer to WHO-GMP standards, raising the bar significantly for facility compliance across India.

✅

What AYUSH GMP Enables

Without AYUSH GMP, a manufacturer cannot legally produce Ayurvedic products for sale in India. With it, your products qualify for state drug licensing, FSSAI product approval (for food-category Ayurvedic supplements), e-commerce marketplace listing on Amazon, Flipkart, and 1mg, export documentation for international markets, and marketing claims backed by regulatory compliance. AYUSH GMP is not optional — it is the legal minimum for any Ayurvedic product sold commercially in India.

Traditional Dosage Forms

Classical Ayurvedic Formulations We Manufacture

Bionial manufactures the complete range of classical Ayurvedic dosage forms as described in the Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI) and the Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API), combining traditional preparation methods with modern quality control.

🌿 Churna (Herbal Powders)
Sanskrit: "that which is finely ground"

Churna preparations are finely ground herbal powders made from single herbs or combinations of dried botanical materials. The herbs are cleaned, dried to specified moisture content, powdered through controlled mesh grinding, and blended in classical proportions. Churnas are among the most versatile Ayurvedic dosage forms — used both as standalone medicines and as base ingredients for other preparations. Bionial manufactures Churna with particle size control, moisture analysis, and microbial testing for every batch.

Triphala Churna Ashwagandha Churna Trikatu Churna Sitopaladi Churna
🍵 Kwath (Decoctions)
Sanskrit: "that which is boiled"

Kwath (Kashaya) preparations are concentrated aqueous decoctions made by boiling coarsely ground herbs in water until the volume reduces to one-quarter or one-eighth of the original, depending on the classical formula. The resulting concentrate captures water-soluble active compounds in a highly bioavailable liquid form. Bionial also produces Kwath in dried tablet form for modern consumer convenience — concentrated decoction spray-dried and compressed into tablets that dissolve in warm water.

Dashamoola Kwath Mahasudarshan Kwath Pathyadi Kwath Immunity Kwath
💊 Vati & Gutika (Herbal Tablets)
Sanskrit: "vartula" — round, spherical

Vati (tablets) and Gutika (pills) are solid oral dosage forms made by combining herbal powders with binding agents (typically honey, jaggery, or gum acacia), shaping into uniform tablets, and drying under controlled conditions. In classical Ayurveda, Vati preparations are preferred for herbs that require precise dosing and extended shelf life. Bionial manufactures Vati on modern tablet compression equipment while maintaining the classical ingredient ratios and processing methods.

Chandraprabha Vati Arogyavardhini Vati Kaishore Guggulu Yograj Guggulu
🏺 Arishta & Asava (Fermented)
Arishta from decoctions; Asava from cold infusions

Arishta and Asava are self-generated alcoholic preparations unique to Ayurveda. Arishta is prepared by fermenting herbal decoctions with jaggery and Dhataki flowers (Woodfordia fruticosa), while Asava uses cold infusions. The natural fermentation produces 5–10% self-generated alcohol that acts as both a preservative and a solvent for extracting additional active compounds. These preparations have long shelf lives and excellent bioavailability due to the alcohol-mediated extraction.

Dashmularishta Ashwagandharishta Drakshasava Kumaryasava
🍯 Avaleha & Paka (Herbal Jams)
Sanskrit: "that which is licked" — semi-solid confections

Avaleha (Lehya) and Paka preparations are semi-solid herbal confections made by cooking herbal decoctions or juices with sugar, jaggery, or honey until a thick jam-like consistency is achieved. The most widely known Avaleha is Chyawanprash — a polyherbal rasayana containing 40+ herbs. These preparations combine the therapeutic benefits of herbs with palatability, making them ideal for daily use formulations and Ayurvedic immunity products. Bionial manufactures Avaleha with Brix monitoring, pH control, and microbial testing.

Chyawanprash Brahma Rasayana Agastya Haritaki Vasavaleha
🌲 Guggulu Preparations
From Commiphora wightii — oleo-gum resin

Guggulu preparations are a special class of Ayurvedic formulations based on purified Guggulu (Commiphora wightii resin) combined with specific herbal powders. The Guggulu must first undergo Shodhana (purification) — traditionally processed with Triphala Kwath — before being combined with other ingredients. These formulations are central to Ayurvedic management of joint health, inflammation, cholesterol, and metabolic conditions. Bionial performs Guggulu Shodhana in-house following classical protocols with modern quality verification.

Yograj Guggulu Kaishore Guggulu Triphala Guggulu Medohar Guggulu
🔥 Bhasma (Calcined Mineral/Metal Preparations)
Sanskrit: "ash" — incinerated to nano-particle form

Bhasma preparations are calcined mineral, metal, or marine-origin materials that have been subjected to repeated cycles of Shodhana (purification) and Marana (incineration) until they are reduced to extremely fine, biocompatible particles. Classical texts describe Bhasma as achieving "Varitara" (floating on water) and "Rekhapurna" (filling finger creases) — indicating nano-to-micro particle size and extremely high surface area. Common Bhasma preparations include Praval Pishti (coral calcium), Godanti Bhasma (gypsum), and Mukta Pishti (pearl calcium). Bhasma manufacturing requires specialised equipment, trained practitioners, and rigorous heavy metal safety testing — Bionial's facility handles select Bhasma preparations with full analytical validation including XRD, particle size analysis, and toxic element screening to ensure safety.

Restricted Herb Compliance

Schedule E(1) Herb Handling Capability

Schedule E(1) of the Drugs & Cosmetics Act lists potent Ayurvedic herbs that require specialised handling, dosage limits, and cautionary labelling. Bionial is equipped to handle these restricted botanicals in full regulatory compliance.

What Is Schedule E(1)?

Schedule E(1) is a regulatory schedule under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940 that lists Ayurvedic herbs and minerals considered potent or potentially toxic in improper doses. Products containing these ingredients must carry specific cautionary statements on the label, such as "To be taken under medical supervision" and must adhere to strict maximum dosage limits prescribed in the schedule. Many classical Ayurvedic formulations — including some of the most therapeutically important ones — contain Schedule E(1) herbs, making compliance capability essential for any serious Ayurvedic manufacturer.

Bionial’s Capability

Our facility has the trained personnel, documented standard operating procedures, and quality control infrastructure to process Schedule E(1) herbs safely and in full compliance with the law. This includes performing traditional Shodhana (purification) under documented, controlled conditions; maintaining segregated, restricted-access inventory storage for Schedule E(1) raw materials; implementing in-process dosage verification to ensure finished products stay within prescribed limits; and generating compliant label copy with all required cautionary statements.

It ensures premises, equipment, personnel, raw material handling, quality control, and documentation meet the standards prescribed under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940.
