Our Story

Building India's Next-Generation Nutraceutical Manufacturing Platform

Bionial Lifesciences Pvt. Ltd. was established with a singular purpose: to accelerate innovation in the Indian nutraceutical and Ayurvedic supplements industry by offering world-class contract manufacturing that is fast, compliant, and accessible to brands of every size.

Operating from a purpose-built 10,000 sq ft facility in Sector-82 of Mohali's JLPL Industrial Area, Bionial bridges the gap between global-grade manufacturing standards and the agility that emerging supplement brands need. Our facility houses dedicated production lines for capsules, tablets, softgels, syrups, powders, sachets, and classical Ayurvedic dosage forms, all under one roof.

What began as a focused contract manufacturing unit has grown into a full-spectrum nutraceutical partner. Today, Bionial serves over 100 brands across India, from D2C wellness startups launching their first SKU to established pharmaceutical companies expanding into nutraceuticals. Our catalogue of 218+ ready-to-manufacture formulations, including 50+ featuring globally patented ingredients, enables brands to go from concept to market in as little as 30 days.

Our Facility

10,000 Sq Ft WHO-GMP Certified Manufacturing Facility

Our facility in Mohali's JLPL Industrial Area has been designed ground-up for multi-dosage-form nutraceutical and Ayurvedic manufacturing. The layout follows WHO-GMP zoning principles with clearly demarcated raw material quarantine, production, packaging, and finished goods storage areas.

  • Dedicated capsule filling, tablet compression, and softgel encapsulation lines
  • Liquid manufacturing and syrup bottling section with stainless steel vessels
  • Powder blending and sachet filling with ribbon blenders and multi-head weighers
  • Temperature and humidity-controlled raw material and finished goods warehouse
  • In-house QC laboratory with HPLC, UV-Vis spectrophotometer, dissolution apparatus, and microbiology testing setup
  • Separate AYUSH-licensed production area for classical Ayurvedic formulations

The facility operates a HVAC system with HEPA-filtered air handling units, maintaining Class 100,000 cleanroom conditions in production zones. All equipment is validated and calibrated per WHO-GMP schedule, and every production batch is manufactured under documented Standard Operating Procedures.

Certifications & Compliance

Manufacturing Under International Quality Standards

Bionial Lifesciences holds multiple certifications that validate our commitment to manufacturing excellence, product safety, and regulatory compliance.

✅

WHO-GMP Certification

Our manufacturing facility is certified under WHO Good Manufacturing Practice guidelines, the global gold standard for pharmaceutical and nutraceutical production. This certification covers facility design, equipment qualification, process validation, quality control, and documentation practices across all dosage forms we manufacture.

✅

FSSAI License

Bionial holds a Central FSSAI License for the manufacture of nutraceutical, dietary supplement, and health supplement products. All labels, claims, and packaging materials are reviewed for FSSAI compliance before production, and every batch carries the mandatory FSSAI logo and license number.

✅

AYUSH GMP License

Our dedicated Ayurvedic manufacturing section operates under a valid AYUSH GMP license issued by the Punjab State Drug Controller. This license enables us to manufacture classical and proprietary Ayurvedic formulations including churnas, tablets, capsules, and liquid extracts under GMP conditions.

✅

ISO 22000 & HACCP

Our food safety management systems follow ISO 22000:2018 and HACCP principles. Critical control points are identified and monitored throughout the manufacturing process, from raw material receipt through production, packaging, and dispatch.

✅

GLP (Good Laboratory Practice)

Our in-house quality control laboratory follows GLP standards for all analytical testing. Instruments are calibrated per schedule, methods are validated, and all test results are documented with full traceability from raw material to finished product.

✅

Batch Documentation & Traceability

Every production batch at Bionial comes with a complete Batch Manufacturing Record (BMR), Certificate of Analysis (COA), stability data, and material traceability documentation. This enables our brand partners to meet regulatory queries and consumer transparency demands with confidence.
