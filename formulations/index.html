What Are Ready-to-Manufacture Nutraceutical Formulations?

Ready-to-manufacture nutraceutical formulations are pre-validated product recipes that a contract manufacturer has already developed, stability-tested, and approved for immediate production. Bionial Lifesciences maintains a catalogue of 218 such formulations across 15 therapeutic categories including bone and joint, cognitive health, sports nutrition, Ayurvedic, and liposomal delivery. Each formula is developed with pharmacopoeially-approved excipients, validated for bioavailability, and produced under WHO-GMP, FSSAI, and AYUSH GMP certification at our Mohali, Punjab facility. Brands selecting from this catalogue eliminate 3 to 6 months of R&D time and can move directly to pilot batches. Formulas marked with a star contain clinically validated patented APIs such as KSM-66 Ashwagandha, Kaneka CoQ10, BioPerine, LactoSpore, and BacoMind that carry published clinical evidence and intellectual property protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many formulations does Bionial have in its catalogue?

218+ ready-to-manufacture formulations across 15 therapeutic categories, including 79 formulas with patented ingredients such as KSM-66, Kaneka CoQ10, BioPerine, LactoSpore, and BacoMind.

Can I customize an existing formulation?

Yes. We can adjust dosages, combine multiple formulations, or substitute specific ingredients while maintaining WHO-GMP compliance. Contact us with your requirements.

What does a patented formula mean in the catalogue?

Patented formulas contain branded, clinically-studied APIs such as KSM-66 Ashwagandha, Kaneka CoQ10, BioPerine, LactoSpore, or BacoMind with published clinical evidence and intellectual property protection.

How quickly can production begin for a catalogue formulation?

Catalogue formulations move to production in 25-30 days from order confirmation including raw material procurement, batch manufacturing, QC testing, and dispatch.

Do you provide Certificate of Analysis for all formulations?

Yes. Every batch comes with a full CoA including identity, potency, microbial, heavy metals, and dissolution testing from our in-house quality lab.
