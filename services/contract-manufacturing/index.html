Understanding Contract Manufacturing

What is Nutraceutical Contract Manufacturing?

Nutraceutical contract manufacturing is a business model in which a brand outsources the formulation, production, quality control, and packaging of dietary supplements, health foods, and Ayurvedic products to a licensed, GMP-certified facility. This allows brands to focus on marketing, sales, and distribution while a specialist manufacturer like Bionial Lifesciences handles the entire production chain — from raw material sourcing and regulatory compliance to batch testing and dispatch. India's nutraceutical industry is projected to reach $18 billion by 2025, according to ASSOCHAM, driven by growing demand from D2C brands and pharma companies outsourcing production. By partnering with a WHO-GMP certified facility in Mohali, Punjab, brands gain access to world-class infrastructure, patented APIs, and regulatory expertise without the capital expenditure of building their own manufacturing unit.

End-to-End Process

What's Included in Every Contract

When you partner with Bionial, you get a complete, transparent manufacturing process — from initial NDA to final dispatch. Here is what every engagement includes.

1

NDA & Formulation Review

We begin with a mutual non-disclosure agreement to protect your intellectual property. Our R&D team reviews your brief, evaluates ingredient compatibility, dosage feasibility, and regulatory compliance before proposing a final formulation within 2–3 business days.

2

Ready-Stock or Custom Formula

Choose from our library of 218+ validated, ready-to-manufacture formulations — or bring your own formula. Our R&D team can also develop custom formulations from scratch, incorporating patented APIs like KSM-66, BioPerine, or Cognizin to differentiate your product in the market.

3

Pilot Batch (Day 4–7)

A small-scale pilot batch is produced in our WHO-GMP facility to validate the formulation, check tablet hardness, capsule fill weights, syrup viscosity, or softgel integrity. This step ensures the formula performs as designed before committing to full-scale production.

4

Sample Approval & CoA

Physical samples are shipped to you along with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) covering identity, potency, heavy metals, microbial limits, and disintegration time. You review, request modifications if needed, and give final sign-off before production begins.

5

Full Production with QC Release

Once approved, we move to full-scale manufacturing. Every batch undergoes in-process quality checks (weight variation, hardness, friability, dissolution) and final QC release testing. Finished goods are packaged, labeled, and prepared for dispatch within the 30-day timeline.

6

FSSAI-Compliant Batch Documentation

Every order ships with complete regulatory documentation: batch manufacturing record (BMR), batch packaging record (BPR), Certificate of Analysis, stability data, and FSSAI-compliant product labels. This documentation package ensures you are audit-ready from Day 1.
