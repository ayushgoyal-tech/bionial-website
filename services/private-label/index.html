THE OPPORTUNITY

What Is Private Label Nutraceutical Manufacturing?

Private label nutraceutical manufacturing is a business model where a contract manufacturer produces dietary supplements, vitamins, herbal extracts, and functional health products that are branded and sold under your company's name. Instead of investing in your own manufacturing facility, raw material sourcing, regulatory filings, and quality-control infrastructure, you partner with a certified manufacturer like Bionial Lifesciences who handles the entire production pipeline. You select formulations from our ready catalog or request custom modifications, supply your branding assets, and receive finished, market-ready products shipped to your warehouse. The private label nutraceutical market in India is growing at 18% CAGR as brands seek faster time-to-market without investing in manufacturing infrastructure. This model is especially popular with D2C wellness startups, pharmacy chains, Ayurvedic brands, and e-commerce sellers who want to offer a curated supplement range under their own name without the capital expenditure and regulatory complexity of in-house production.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Private Label Manufacturing FAQ

How do I choose a private label formula?

Browse our catalog of 218+ ready formulations by category (bone health, women's health, brain & neuro, sports, Ayurvedic, etc.). Our R&D team can also recommend formulations based on your target market and trending ingredients. Whether you want a bestselling multivitamin, a niche nootropic stack, or a trending Ayurvedic wellness product, we have a ready formula or can customize one to match your vision.

Can I put my brand name on a Bionial formula?

Absolutely. Private label manufacturing means our formulations carry your brand name, logo, and packaging design. We handle FSSAI-compliant label design and printing. Bionial operates strictly as your behind-the-scenes manufacturing partner — your customers will see only your brand identity on the finished product, from the bottle to the box to the Certificate of Analysis.

Do you provide packaging for private label orders?

Yes. We offer bottles, blister packs, sachet filling, jar packaging, and custom gift boxes. Packaging design assistance and FSSAI label compliance review are included. You can choose from our standard packaging options or bring your own packaging materials for a fully custom presentation. Our team ensures the packaging meets all regulatory and shelf-life requirements.

How long does private label manufacturing take?

For ready formulations, 21–30 business days from purchase order to dispatch. This includes label design approval (days 1–5), raw material procurement and blending (days 6–15), manufacturing and in-process QC (days 16–22), final quality testing and packaging (days 23–28), and documentation and dispatch (days 29–30). Custom formulation projects may add 7–10 additional days for R&D and sample approval.

What is the minimum order for private label?

Our minimum order quantity starts at 1,000 units per SKU. This low MOQ is ideal for D2C brands, wellness startups, and companies testing new product lines. Unlike many contract manufacturers who require 5,000–10,000 unit minimums, our 1,000-unit threshold lets you test multiple products, validate market demand, and scale production as your brand grows — without a massive upfront inventory commitment.
