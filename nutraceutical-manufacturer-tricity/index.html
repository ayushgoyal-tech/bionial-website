Definition & Context

What is a Tricity Nutraceutical Manufacturer?

The Tricity refers to the combined urban agglomeration of Chandigarh (Union Territory), Mohali/SAS Nagar (Punjab), and Panchkula (Haryana) — three cities that function as a single interconnected metropolitan ecosystem. Together they represent one of North India's most dynamic economic zones, with a combined population exceeding two million and a strong culture of entrepreneurship, healthcare, and technology.

A Tricity nutraceutical manufacturer is a WHO-GMP certified production facility located within this ecosystem, capable of serving supplement brands, pharma companies, and wellness entrepreneurs across all three cities without the logistics burden of cross-state manufacturing. Bionial Lifesciences at Plot 459, Sector-82, JLPL Industrial Area, Mohali is the preferred partner for Tricity brands because of its central Mohali location, just 30 minutes from Chandigarh Sector 17 and 20 minutes from Panchkula Sector 12, combined with its 218+ validated formulations, 50+ patented APIs, WHO-GMP certification, and a proven 30-day delivery pipeline from brief to finished product.

Bionial's facility in JLPL Industrial Area, Sector-82, Mohali holds WHO-GMP, FSSAI, AYUSH GMP, ISO, GMP, and HACCP certifications — giving Tricity brands the regulatory compliance they need for domestic sales, marketplace listings, and export markets.
