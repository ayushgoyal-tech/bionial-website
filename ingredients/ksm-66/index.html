UNDERSTANDING KSM-66

What Is KSM-66 Ashwagandha?

KSM-66 is a branded, full-spectrum ashwagandha root extract manufactured by Ixoreal Biomed Inc., a company headquartered in Hyderabad, India, with research operations in the United States. The name “KSM-66” refers to a proprietary extraction process that took 14 years of research and development to perfect — a process that uses only the roots of the Withania somnifera plant, never the leaves or stems, and employs a unique “Green Chemistry” extraction method based on the principles of pre-treatment with milk, without using alcohol or any chemical solvents. This root-only approach is significant because Ayurvedic tradition has always used ashwagandha root, and the root contains the most balanced and bioactive profile of withanolides, the key pharmacological compounds responsible for ashwagandha’s therapeutic effects.

KSM-66 is standardized to contain a minimum of 5% withanolides as measured by HPLC (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography), ensuring consistent potency across every batch. Unlike generic ashwagandha powders — which may contain anywhere from 1% to 2.5% withanolides with significant batch-to-batch variation — KSM-66 delivers a guaranteed, reproducible dose of bioactive compounds in every serving. The extract also maintains the full spectrum of naturally occurring co-factors, including withanolide glycosides, withaferin A, alkaloids, and amino acids, which work synergistically to produce the adaptogenic effects documented in clinical literature.

What sets KSM-66 apart from every other ashwagandha extract on the market is the sheer volume and quality of its clinical evidence. While most ashwagandha brands rely on one or two small studies, KSM-66 has been the subject of 48 double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled human clinical trials conducted at leading research institutions across India, the United States, Europe, and Australia. These trials have investigated KSM-66 across a wide range of health outcomes — from stress and anxiety reduction to testosterone enhancement, muscle strength, sleep quality, cognitive function, thyroid health, and weight management — with consistently positive results published in peer-reviewed journals.

For supplement brands and contract manufacturing partners seeking to formulate premium adaptogen products, KSM-66 represents the safest, most evidence-based choice in the ashwagandha category. Its extensive patent portfolio, GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe) self-affirmed status, organic certification (USDA and EU), kosher and halal certifications, and widespread consumer recognition make it the industry standard for serious ashwagandha formulations.

KSM-66 is backed by 48 double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled human clinical trials — more than any other ashwagandha extract on the market. This makes it the most clinically validated adaptogen available to formulators and supplement brands worldwide.

PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Clinical Evidence Behind KSM-66

Four landmark clinical trials that established KSM-66 as the most evidence-based ashwagandha extract available to formulators worldwide.

Chandrasekhar et al., 2012

Stress & Cortisol Reduction

Indian Journal of Psychological Medicine, 34(3), 255–262

A 60-day, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial involving 64 adults with a history of chronic stress. Subjects receiving 600mg/day of KSM-66 demonstrated a 27.9% reduction in serum cortisol levels compared to placebo. The treatment group also showed significant improvements across all stress-assessment scales — the PSS (Perceived Stress Scale), GHQ-28 (General Health Questionnaire), and DASS (Depression Anxiety Stress Scale). No adverse effects were reported, establishing KSM-66 as a safe, effective cortisol-modulating adaptogen for daily use.

Wankhede et al., 2015

Testosterone & Muscle Strength

Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition, 12, 43

An 8-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial with 57 young male subjects (18–50 years) engaged in resistance training. The KSM-66 group (600mg/day) experienced significantly greater increases in muscle strength (bench press: +44kg vs. +26.4kg; leg extension: +14.5kg vs. +9.8kg), muscle size (arm: +8.6 cm² vs. +5.3 cm²), testosterone elevation (96.2 ng/dL increase vs. 18.0 ng/dL), and greater reduction in exercise-induced muscle damage (creatine kinase levels) and body fat percentage compared to placebo.

Langade et al., 2019

Sleep Quality Improvement

PLOS ONE, 14(12), e0226220

A 10-week, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial with 150 healthy adults reporting non-restorative sleep. The KSM-66 group (600mg/day) showed statistically significant improvements in sleep onset latency (falling asleep faster), total sleep time, sleep efficiency (percentage of time in bed actually sleeping), and sleep quality as measured by actigraphy (wrist-worn sleep monitors) and validated questionnaires (Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index). Both healthy subjects and those with insomnia benefited, with the insomnia subgroup showing even more pronounced improvements.

Lopresti et al., 2019

Female Hormonal Balance

Journal of Evidence-Based Integrative Medicine, 24

An 8-week, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial with 52 women aged 21–50 experiencing symptoms of hormonal imbalance. Women receiving 600mg/day of KSM-66 showed significant improvements in the Female Sexual Function Index (FSFI), including arousal, lubrication, orgasm, and satisfaction domains. The treatment group also demonstrated measurable improvements in serum testosterone and DHEA-S levels, suggesting that KSM-66 supports the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis in women, helping normalize hormone production affected by chronic stress. This study opened the door for KSM-66 in women’s health, PCOS support, and hormonal balance formulations.
