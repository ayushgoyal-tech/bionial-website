Definition & Background

What is Kaneka CoQ10?

Kaneka CoQ10 is coenzyme Q10 manufactured by Kaneka Corporation, a Japanese specialty chemicals company headquartered in Osaka, Japan. Kaneka pioneered the commercial production of CoQ10 in 1977 and remains the world's largest and most scientifically credible producer of both ubiquinone (Kaneka Q10™) and ubiquinol (Kaneka QH™) — the two biologically active forms of coenzyme Q10 used in dietary supplements worldwide.

What distinguishes Kaneka CoQ10 from generic alternatives is its proprietary yeast fermentation process, which produces a CoQ10 molecule that is bioidentical to what the human body synthesises naturally. The fermentation process achieves purity greater than 98%, independently verified through HPLC testing, and confirmed by Kaneka's proprietary QH mark certification for its ubiquinol form. Ubiquinone is the oxidised form that the body converts for cellular energy use, while ubiquinol is the reduced, active antioxidant form especially important for adults over 40. For supplement brands seeking a clinically referenced, scientifically credible CoQ10 ingredient, Kaneka is the global gold standard.

Bionial Lifesciences uses authentic Kaneka CoQ10 from Kaneka's certified supply chain. Every batch at our WHO-GMP Mohali facility ships with Kaneka's ingredient CoA plus independent NABL-accredited third-party purity verification — two layers of quality assurance on every order.

It is the most cost-effective Kaneka CoQ10 form and the most extensively studied in peer-reviewed clinical trials for cardiovascular support, mitochondrial function, and statin-associated CoQ10 depletion.

Kaneka QH™

Kaneka QH (Ubiquinol)

Reduced Active Form • Enhanced Bioavailability

The reduced, active antioxidant form of CoQ10 — the form the body actually uses in mitochondrial energy production and free radical defence. As adults age past 40, conversion efficiency from ubiquinone to ubiquinol naturally declines. Kaneka QH™ bypasses this conversion entirely, making it the preferred ingredient for premium anti-ageing, cardiovascular, and senior health supplement lines.

  • Higher bioavailability compared to ubiquinone
  • Carries Kaneka's proprietary QH mark certification
  • Preferred for adults 40+ and premium senior product lines
  • Commands higher retail price point vs. ubiquinone
  • Available as softgels and capsules

Frequently Asked Questions — Kaneka CoQ10

Technical and commercial questions about manufacturing with Kaneka CoQ10 answered by our formulation team.

What is the difference between Kaneka Q10 and Kaneka QH?

Kaneka Q10 is ubiquinone, the oxidised form of CoQ10. Kaneka QH is ubiquinol, the reduced (active antioxidant) form. The body converts ubiquinone to ubiquinol before use in the mitochondrial electron transport chain. Ubiquinol (QH) is generally considered more bioavailable, particularly for individuals over 40 whose conversion efficiency decreases with age. Both forms use authentic Kaneka-sourced CoQ10. Bionial manufactures both; the choice depends on your target demographic, clinical positioning, and retail price point. Ubiquinone is more cost-effective; ubiquinol commands a premium.

What dosages of CoQ10 do you manufacture?

We manufacture Kaneka CoQ10 formulations from 30 mg to 400 mg per serving. The most common dosages are 100 mg and 200 mg for general cardiovascular and cellular energy support. High-dose formulations at 300–400 mg are available for statin users and clinical nutrition products where therapeutic-level supplementation is indicated. The appropriate dosage is determined during formulation review based on your target indication, consumer profile, and clinical evidence you intend to cite on the product label.

Is Kaneka CoQ10 fermentation-derived?

Yes. Kaneka CoQ10 is produced through a proprietary yeast fermentation process, yielding a molecule bioidentical to the CoQ10 the human body produces naturally. Unlike synthetic CoQ10 derived from chemical synthesis or tobacco-leaf extraction, fermentation-derived Kaneka CoQ10 achieves purity greater than 98% and is considered superior in bioavailability. The fermentation origin also makes it inherently suitable for vegan and vegetarian supplement formulations — an important differentiator for brands targeting plant-based or lifestyle-focused consumers.

Each batch manufactured with Kaneka CoQ10 at our Mohali WHO-GMP facility is accompanied by: a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from NABL-accredited third-party laboratories confirming finished product identity and purity; Kaneka's own ingredient CoA confirming raw material specifications; batch manufacturing records; WHO-GMP batch release documentation; and FSSAI-compliant labelling information.
