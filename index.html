About Bionial

Built for Brands That Mean Business

Bionial Lifesciences is a WHO-GMP certified nutraceutical contract manufacturer based in JLPL Industrial Area, Sector-82, Mohali — at the heart of North India's pharmaceutical corridor. We partner with D2C startups, established health brands, and export-focused companies to manufacture full-range supplements and Ayurvedic products with precision.

Our 30-day delivery commitment, transparent pricing, and startup-friendly MOQs make premium manufacturing accessible to brands at every stage. From formulation to finished product — we handle it all.
