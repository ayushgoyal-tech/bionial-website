Regional Context

The Baddi-Mohali Pharma Corridor

How Mohali's position in India's pharmaceutical belt benefits your nutraceutical manufacturing.

The stretch from Baddi (Himachal Pradesh) to Mohali (Punjab) represents one of India's most concentrated pharmaceutical manufacturing corridors. Baddi alone accounts for approximately 35% of India's pharmaceutical production, and the corridor extends southward through Mohali, creating a supply chain ecosystem that nutraceutical manufacturers can leverage for raw material sourcing, excipient procurement, and packaging materials.

Bionial Lifesciences at Plot 459, JLPL Industrial Area, sits at the southern anchor of this corridor. This means our procurement team can source pharmaceutical-grade raw materials — from magnesium stearate to HPMC capsule shells — from vendors who are 30 to 90 minutes away. The proximity reduces shipping costs, shortens lead times, and enables our quality team to conduct in-person vendor audits without air travel.

For brands looking for a manufacturing partner, the Mohali location offers an additional advantage: regulatory familiarity. The Punjab FDA and FSSAI regional offices are well-versed in nutraceutical licensing requirements, and the local regulatory ecosystem is established and predictable. This reduces the time and complexity of obtaining manufacturing licenses, product approvals, and export documentation.

Furthermore, Mohali's pharmaceutical heritage means that contract testing laboratories (NABL-accredited), stability study facilities, and packaging vendors are all available locally. Bionial sends every batch for third-party testing to NABL-accredited labs within Mohali, and results typically come back within 3-5 business days — compared to 7-10 days when using labs in distant cities.
