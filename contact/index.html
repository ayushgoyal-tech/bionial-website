HOW IT WORKS

How to Start Contract Manufacturing with Bionial

Starting your nutraceutical brand with Bionial Lifesciences is a straightforward process designed for speed and transparency. Begin by submitting an enquiry through our contact form, email, or WhatsApp with your product concept, preferred dosage form, and estimated quantity. Our R&D team reviews your requirements and responds within 24 hours with a preliminary proposal and pricing. Once you approve, we prepare a bench sample within 5–7 days for your evaluation. After sample approval and purchase order, our WHO-GMP certified facility in Mohali begins pilot batch manufacturing followed by full-scale production with complete quality control testing. Every batch comes with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA), stability data, and FSSAI-compliant documentation. The entire cycle from first contact to dispatch takes 30 days for ready formulations and 45–60 days for custom formulations. Whether you need capsules, tablets, softgels, syrups, powders, or Ayurvedic products, our team guides you through every step of the manufacturing journey.
